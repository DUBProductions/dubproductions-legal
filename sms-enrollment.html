D.U.B Productions DJ SMS Enrollment

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

This page explains how D.U.B Productions obtains consent before enrolling members of its private professional DJ roster in recurring operational SMS communications.

Who Receives Messages?

D.U.B Productions maintains a private roster of professional DJs. Operational text messages are sent only to DJs who have been personally recruited by D.U.B Productions and who have affirmatively agreed to receive operational SMS communications.

How the Enrollment Process Works

Step 1: D.U.B Productions personally recruits a DJ to join its private professional roster.

Step 2: During the onboarding process, D.U.B Productions explains its operational SMS messaging program and asks whether the DJ agrees to receive operational text messages.

Step 3: The DJ is informed that operational messages may include:

  • Availability requests
  • Booking offers
  • Booking confirmations
  • Event reminders
  • Schedule changes
  • Assignment updates

The DJ is also informed that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and recipients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

Step 4: The DJ must provide affirmative consent before the mobile phone number is enrolled in DUBPRO operational messaging. SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of joining the D.U.B Productions roster.

Step 5: After affirmative consent is received, D.U.B Productions enrolls the DJ in DUBPRO operational messaging using the D.U.B Productions business phone number.

Consent Language Provided During Onboarding

D.U.B Productions requests permission to send recurring operational text messages regarding DJ availability requests, booking offers, booking confirmations, event reminders, schedule changes, and assignment updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Recipients may reply STOP to opt out at any time or HELP for assistance.

SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of joining the D.U.B Productions roster. The DJ must provide affirmative consent before being enrolled in operational SMS communications.

Purpose of SMS Communications

Operational SMS messages are used solely to coordinate DJ scheduling, event staffing, booking management, and assignment updates. D.U.B Productions does not send marketing or promotional SMS messages.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on DJ availability, scheduled events, booking activity, and operational needs.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's wireless carrier and mobile service plan.

Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance or contact D.U.B Productions directly.

Privacy and Data Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, and text-message opt-in information are never sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
