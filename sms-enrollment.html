D.U.B Productions DJ SMS Enrollment

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

This page explains the SMS enrollment process used by D.U.B Productions for members of its private professional DJ roster.

Who Receives Messages?

D.U.B Productions personally recruits DJs through direct professional relationships. SMS messages are sent only to DJs who have been invited to join the D.U.B Productions roster and who have affirmatively agreed to receive operational text messages.

How the Enrollment Process Works

Step 1: D.U.B Productions personally contacts a DJ and invites the DJ to join its private roster.

Step 2: The DJ provides their mobile phone number directly to D.U.B Productions for onboarding and scheduling purposes.

Step 3: Before the DJ is enrolled in recurring operational SMS communications, the DJ receives a consent request explaining the types of messages, message frequency, possible carrier charges, and how to obtain help or opt out.

Step 4: The DJ must reply YES to provide affirmative consent.

Step 5: Only after the affirmative reply is received is the DJ enrolled in DUBPRO operational messaging.

Consent Request Shown to DJs

D.U.B Productions: Before you're added to DUBPRO, reply YES to receive operational texts about DJ bookings, availability, schedules, reminders, and updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

Types of Messages

  • DJ availability requests
  • Booking offers
  • Booking confirmations
  • Event reminders
  • Schedule changes
  • Assignment updates

These messages are used solely for operational scheduling and event staffing. D.U.B Productions does not use this messaging program for marketing or promotional messages.

Message Frequency and Carrier Charges

Message frequency varies depending on scheduling activity, booking opportunities, and event assignments. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out and Help

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance.
