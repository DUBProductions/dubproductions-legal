D.U.B Productions DJ SMS Enrollment

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

This page explains how D.U.B Productions obtains consent before enrolling members of its private professional DJ roster in recurring operational SMS communications.

Who Receives Messages?

D.U.B Productions personally recruits DJs through direct professional relationships. SMS messages are sent only to DJs who have been invited to join the D.U.B Productions roster and who have affirmatively agreed to receive operational text messages.

How the Enrollment Process Works

Step 1: D.U.B Productions personally contacts a DJ and invites the DJ to join its private professional roster.

Step 2: During a direct onboarding conversation by phone or in person, D.U.B Productions asks whether the DJ agrees to receive recurring operational SMS messages.

Step 3: The DJ is informed that the messages may include availability requests, booking offers, booking confirmations, event reminders, schedule changes, and assignment updates.

The DJ is also informed that message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and recipients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

Step 4: The DJ must affirmatively agree before their mobile phone number is enrolled in DUBPRO operational messaging. SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of joining the D.U.B Productions roster.

Step 5: D.U.B Productions records the DJ's consent and then enrolls the DJ to receive operational SMS communications from the D.U.B Productions business phone number.

Consent Language Provided During Onboarding

D.U.B Productions would like to send you recurring operational text messages concerning DJ availability requests, booking offers, booking confirmations, event reminders, schedule changes, and assignment updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

SMS consent is voluntary and is not a condition of joining the D.U.B Productions roster. Do you agree to receive these messages?

Types of Messages

  • DJ availability requests
  • Booking offers
  • Booking confirmations
  • Event reminders
  • Schedule changes
  • Assignment updates

These messages are used solely for operational scheduling, event staffing, and assignment coordination. D.U.B Productions does not use this messaging program for marketing or promotional messages.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending on scheduling activity, booking opportunities, and event assignments.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the recipient's wireless carrier and mobile service plan.

Opt-Out

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS communications.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance or contact D.U.B Productions using the information below.

Privacy and Data Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent records, and text-message opt-in information are not sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
