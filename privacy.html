D.U.B Productions Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

D.U.B Productions respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, mobile phone number, scheduling information, availability information, and other details needed to manage DJ assignments and event services.

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used for operational and business communications, including:

  • DJ availability requests
  • Booking offers and confirmations
  • Schedule confirmations
  • Event reminders
  • Schedule changes
  • Assignment updates
  • Other event-related operational communications

SMS Communications and Consent

D.U.B Productions sends operational text messages only to DJs who voluntarily join the D.U.B Productions entertainment roster and expressly agree during onboarding to receive SMS communications.

During onboarding, DJs voluntarily provide their mobile phone number and are informed that they may receive text messages from D.U.B Productions regarding:

  • Availability requests
  • Booking offers
  • Booking confirmations
  • Event reminders
  • Schedule changes
  • Assignment-related operational updates

These text messages are used solely for operational scheduling and business communications. They are not marketing or promotional messages.

Message frequency varies based on scheduling activity, booking activity, and event assignments. Message and data rates may apply.

Recipients may reply STOP at any time to opt out of SMS communications.

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance or contact D.U.B Productions at a.dubproduction@gmail.com .

Mobile Information and Data Sharing

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, and text-message opt-in information will not be sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Personal information may be used only as necessary to operate D.U.B Productions, manage DJ scheduling, communicate about assignments, and comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

Data Security

D.U.B Productions takes reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

Your Choices

You may request that your contact information be updated or removed by contacting D.U.B Productions.

You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying STOP.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

D.U.B Productions may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
