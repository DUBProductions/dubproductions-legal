D.U.B Productions SMS Consent

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

This page explains how D.U.B Productions obtains consent before sending operational text messages to members of the D.U.B Productions DJ roster.

Who Receives Messages?

Only DJs who voluntarily join the D.U.B Productions entertainment roster and provide their mobile phone number during onboarding.

How Do DJs Opt In?

During onboarding, DJs are asked whether they wish to receive operational text messages from D.U.B Productions.

By voluntarily providing their mobile phone number and agreeing during onboarding, DJs consent to receive SMS communications regarding:

  • Availability Requests
  • Booking Offers
  • Booking Confirmations
  • Event Reminders
  • Schedule Changes
  • Operational Updates

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies depending upon scheduling activity and event assignments.

Message & Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending upon your wireless carrier.

Opt-Out

Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Help

Recipients may reply HELP for assistance or contact:

a.dubproduction@gmail.com

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. SMS communications are used solely for operational scheduling and business communications.
